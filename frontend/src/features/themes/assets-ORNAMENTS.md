# Original ornament artwork

The following vector artwork additions were drawn directly in SVG for Kuren on
2026-11-15. They contain no stock, downloaded, traced, or third-party imagery:

- `traditional/components/TradCrest.vue`: symmetrical fan, leaves and curling filigree.
- `traditional/components/TradCorner.vue`: original geometric and botanical corner engraving.
- `conventional/components/ConvBouquet.vue`: new layered rose details, leaf sprays, veins and buds added to the existing bouquet.

These new drawings are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
They may be used, modified and distributed commercially without attribution.
This dedication covers the new vector drawings only, not other repository code,
photos, fonts, trademarks or pre-existing artwork.

The Heritage artwork is a generic creative composition, not an assertion of
regional cultural authenticity or an adaptation of a named ceremonial emblem.

Density contract: Royal Maroon is ornate (crest, engraved corners, bands),
Botanical Romance is balanced (two corner arrangements and section sprigs),
and Modern Minimalist is minimal (type, rules and whitespace, no vector ornament).
Vectors are decorative (`aria-hidden`, not focusable), static and bundled with
their existing lazy-loaded renderer. Mobile sizes are reduced to protect text.
